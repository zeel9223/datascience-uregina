Programs

Choose your path

Bachelor of Science in Data Science

A four-year degree offered together by Computer Science and Mathematics & Statistics.

  • Analytics, machine learning, programming and artificial intelligence
  • Transfer students are welcome if they meet Faculty of Science admission rules
  • Scholarships open to Faculty of Science students
  • Active student societies for events and networking

Master of Science in Computer Science (Data Science)

A professional, two-year, course-based degree run by the Department of Computer Science.

  • 30 credits of coursework, no thesis required
  • Data-focused programming and the data science lifecycle
  • Statistics and machine learning
  • Developed with support from SGI (Saskatchewan Government Insurance)
